## 🚀 Quick Start Roadmap

1.  **Environment Check:** Ensure your GPU drivers (NVIDIA/AMD) are updated to the latest version.
2.  **Initialization:** Run the `_Installer.exe` and select "Full Setup" to include all high-res assets.
3.  **Model Loading:** Choose your preferred style from the pre-loaded model library (Realism, Anime, 3D, etc.).
4.  **Prompting:** Enter your description; the system will automatically generate motion paths.
5.  **Rendering:** Export directly to `.mp4` or `.mkv` with a high bitrate for maximum quality.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting & Optimization

| **Issue** | **Cause** | **Solution** |
|-----------|----------|----------|
| **Low FPS** | Low VRAM availability | Enable "Fast-Mode" in settings. |
| **Artifacts/Noise** | Denoising strength too low | Increase to 0.75 in the generation panel. |
| **Driver Error** | Outdated CUDA cores | Update to the latest NVIDIA Game Ready driver. |
